# TradeAid Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 15, 2026

## 1. Overview
TradeAid provides token intelligence, risk scoring, wallet analysis, and alert features. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

## 2. Data We Collect
- Account information: username, email, hashed password, optional 2FA metadata.
- Usage data: feature usage, request timestamps, and diagnostic error logs.
- Device/app data: app version, platform, push notification token (if enabled).
- Security data: authentication sessions and API key metadata.

TradeAid does not intentionally collect precise geolocation, contacts, photos, or microphone data.

## 3. How We Use Data
- Provide core app functionality and account management.
- Deliver wallet/token alerts and push notifications (if enabled).
- Improve reliability, prevent abuse, and secure accounts.
- Meet legal and regulatory obligations.

## 4. Push Notifications
If you opt in, TradeAid stores your push token to send product alerts and account security notifications. You can disable notifications in device settings.

## 5. Data Sharing
We do not sell personal data. We may share limited data with:
- Infrastructure providers (hosting, logging, notification delivery).
- Security and fraud-prevention services.
- Legal authorities when required by law.

## 6. Data Retention
We retain account and operational records only as long as needed for service operation, legal obligations, and security purposes.

## 7. Security
We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, including encrypted transport and secure credential handling. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

## 8. Your Rights
Depending on your jurisdiction, you may request access, correction, deletion, or export of your personal data.

## 9. Children
TradeAid is not intended for children under 13 (or equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction).
